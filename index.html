How FreshCheck works

Sensors, cloud platform and dashboard — delivered as one simple monthly service.

01 · SENSE

Sensors go inside

We install sensors in your refrigerators, freezers, cold rooms or refrigerated delivery containers. They read temperature continuously.

02 · ANALYSE

Cloud tracks it

Readings stream to the FreshCheck cloud platform and are stored, so you always have a full temperature history.

03 · ALERT

You act early

Set your own safe range. If a reading goes outside it, FreshCheck alerts you automatically so you can respond immediately.
